## Strongest transferable principles

1. **Make one sentence the scene.** Lex Politica reaches 10vw and lets “The Firm of Record” share a plane with the Capitol (`lex-politica/scroll-00.png`, `mobile-390.png`). Bridge currently tops out at 3.8vw. Transfer: promote one truthful phrase per chapter, not every heading, while preserving readable body measures.
2. **Derive the graphic device from the business.** Axinn repeats one decisive cut through hero, practice tiles and footer (`axinn/scroll-00.png`, `scroll-03.png`, `scroll-07.png`). Transfer: Bridge's existing dotted source-to-target trail should cut, connect and hand off chapters instead of remaining a small connector inside components.
3. **Use hard fields to mark a new chapter.** Lex Politica moves from photograph to white to black and back (`scroll-00.png`–`scroll-07.png`); Axinn uses teal, ice and sand fields (`scroll-00.png`–`scroll-07.png`). Transfer: keep Documento/Cabina's approved hard edge and add one deep Documento proof chapter, using existing tokens and verified contrast.
4. **Let evidence occupy visual mass.** Axinn gives practice areas and people whole fields; Lex Politica makes practice names and results structural rows. Transfer: certification steps, language direction and deliverable facts become the composition, not small cards. No invented stats.
5. **Vary the geometry across adjacent chapters.** The stronger sources alternate full-field hero, editorial split, structured rows and a dedicated close. Direct peers repeat card grids and lose hierarchy. Transfer: no two adjacent Bridge sections use the same title-plus-grid arrangement.
6. **Design the close as a final claim.** Axinn's two destination bars and dark identity footer form a deliberate last beat (`scroll-07.png`–`scroll-11.png`); Lex Politica returns to its institutional image (`scroll-10.png`, `scroll-11.png`). Transfer: Bridge closes on source → target → signed result before contact details, rather than contact strip straight into footer.
7. **Mobile needs a composition, not a shrink.** Lex Politica restacks image, claim and mission into one legible scene; Axinn preserves the cut field and editorial switch (`mobile-390.png`). Quastels shows the failure risk when overlays consume the phone. Transfer: remove floating decoration from the hero, reserve space for WhatsApp, and make the signature stage start within the first mobile viewport sequence.

## Largest baseline gaps

1. Maximum type scale is 3.8vw, so no phrase carries a chapter.
2. The signature stages sit inside raised rounded panels rather than merging with the page composition.
3. White and Bruma bands repeat without enough field contrast or geometry change.
4. Certified translation lacks honest imagery, but the layout still reserves conventional image/card roles instead of making language and document structure the visual material.
5. Neither page has a purpose-built final beat before the common footer.

## Feasibility verdict

- **Production-usable:** monumental Literata/Manrope type, existing dotted trail at larger scale, hard token-based fields, document sheets built from HTML/CSS, real source/target copy, structured rules and the existing seal. These need no new dependency or synthetic asset.
- **Usable with care:** one real booth photograph on Home, unchanged and credited internally; it cannot carry Certified Translation.
- **Not usable:** invented document photography, unverified badges/logos, synthetic legal seals, fabricated metrics, WebGL, scroll-jacking, glow-heavy legal-tech styling and third-party chat overlays.

## Specialist rules that changed the proposal

Accessibility keeps the project's 44 px control target, semantic controls, 4.5:1 text pairs, visible focus and complete reduced-motion states. Frontend direction prevents a decorative mega-type treatment from hiding the job: the first viewport must still say certified translation and simultaneous interpreting, and every reference move must map to a user need or verified proof.
