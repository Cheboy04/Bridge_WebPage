Three directions for a refreshed Bridge

Each direction keeps the globe, the dotted trail and the blue, and redraws everything around them. They differ in which client they lead with: legal documents, live interpreting, or both at once. Pick one and Phase 4 turns it into tokens and final logo files.

What the audit found

  • No type systemThe demo mixes Poppins 900, Inter, Arial, a system stack and DM Serif. Neither site has an h1.
  • Color with no sourceYellow #FFD800 and orange CTAs have no root in the logo. White on yellow measures 1.39:1. The logo grey #808285 fails body text at 3.85:1.
  • Dated logo executionChrome radial gradients on "ridge", fine globe line-work that vanishes small, three unrelated typefaces, dirty PNG transparency.
  • Template tellsHard black shadow on the hero photo, emoji pill, empty video block, English footer with "Pricing Plans" and "#" links.
  • Trust left on the tableThe ATA certification sits in the original logo file but on neither site. Years and client counts disagree (16 vs 17+, 500+ vs 750+).
  • Mixed languages"Get in Touch", "Get A Quote" and "Cotiza Ahora" on the same journey. No language switcher.

Worth keeping: the B knocked out of the globe, the dotted trail, the logo blue #1C61AD (6.25:1 with white), the real booth photography, the 3-step process and "Cotiza ahora" as the one call to action.

Leads with legal and notarial work. It reads like a well-made legal document: editorial serif, warm paper, navy ink, and a rust seal color used only where something is certified. The dotted trail becomes the ring of a seal.

Recommendation

Puente. You asked for both audiences weighted equally and a refresh rather than a reinvention. Puente is the only direction that doesn't make one audience feel like a guest. Its sans wordmark is the closest successor to the current one, and the light/dark modes give the future site a ready structure for its two service paths. Elements can move between directions, for example Sello's seal ring for certified documents inside Puente.

The marks above are quick sketches to compare directions, not final artwork. The final mark is redrawn from the original vector file in Phase 4.

  4. Review for accessibility and consistency, then hand off to the website build.
