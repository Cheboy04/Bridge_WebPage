Signature components (version 2)

  • Inicio, hero: switch between Documento and Evento. In Evento, change the channel (ES, EN, FR, PT).
  • Inicio and Traducción certificada: the language-pair checker. "Cotizar este par" pre-fills the quote form.
  • Traducción certificada: drag the before/after handle, or use the arrow keys on it.
  • Interpretación simultánea: the event planner. "Usar este resumen en mi cotización" carries the brief into the event quote form.
  • Every page: the dotted trail draws itself as you scroll, and the footer name cycles through seven languages.

What to look at

  • Whether the two modes feel like one brand, especially moving from Inicio to the dark interpreting page.
  • The voice: usted throughout and "Solicitar cotización" as the main action.
  • The floating WhatsApp button and the contact block at the end of each page. The button is hidden on the quote form pages.
  • Links marked "No incluido en el prototipo" and the event quote form are not built yet.
  • The certification seal and the booth photo are provisional.
