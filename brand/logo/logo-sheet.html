Construction and clear space

One grid: the disc is 112 u across, the B is 44 x 60 u with a 12 u stem, and “ridge” is Manrope 800 set so its cap height equals the B (60 u). The descriptor is Manrope 700 at 13 u, tracking 0.16 em, left edge on the stem of the “r”. Clear space is half the disc radius (28 u) on every side, measured from the outermost ink.

Trail

  • Dots5, round, white
  • Radii2.5 / 3 / 3.5 / 4 / 4.5 u
  • Spacingequal arc, gaps 4.9 to 2.0 u
  • Pathquadratic (14,84) (26,64) (46,57)
  • Last dot to B5.5 u
  • Edge margin3 u to the disc

Disc

  • Diameter112 u
  • Meridians2 ellipses (rx 24, 46) + 1 parallel, 1.7 u, white 30%
  • B positionx 56, y 26 (centred vertically)
  • Slot (DE)135 u wide, same in all 7

Variants on their surfaces

Light and Dark are the colour versions. Mono ink and Mono white are strict one-colour: the B and the trail are true knockouts, so the surface shows through.

04

Seven languages, one slot

The descriptor slot is sized for Übersetzungen. The type size and tracking never change; shorter words simply end earlier. Greek capitals carry no accents.
