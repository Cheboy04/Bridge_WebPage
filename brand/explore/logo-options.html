Logo and certification stamp: options

Six logo options (M1 to M6) and five stamps (S1 to S5), all drawn from the 2010 globe, the dotted trail and the white B. These are sketches to choose from, not final artwork. Reply with your picks, for example M2 + S3.

The trail is the same in every option: dots graded in size, evenly spaced on a curve, never smaller than about 1.4 px across at 32 px. Amber (Rastro) only ever appears on the trail and seal accents.

A

Main logo

Each option shows the horizontal lockup on Bruma, white, Tinta and in one ink colour, then a size test at 64, 32 and 16 px. On the one-colour tile the globe is ink and the B and trail are knocked out.

M1

Refresh fiel

Faithful refresh

Idea. B-as-first-letter lockup. Original globe, three clean meridians, one cleaned trail rising into a solid knocked-out B; "ridge" continues the word.

Weakness. Most literal, so the least new. The B only reads as a letter when the word is beside it; "ridge" alone is not a word.

Descriptor test

M1 lockup, longest word sets the slot

The descriptor sits under "ridge". The slot is sized for Übersetzungen; every language below fits without shrinking the type.

B

Certification stamp

Each stamp works in one colour for a rubber or self-inking stamp: the globe-B is cut out of the disc, and no line is thinner than about 0.3 mm at the stated size. The colour version is for the website and PDFs. Documents tiles are shown at true physical size on a calibrated screen.

S1

Anillo punteado

Dotted ring

Idea. The trail dots become the perimeter, growing from the bottom to the top on both sides. Globe-B in the centre, TRADUCCIÓN above and CERTIFICADA below.

Weakness. Dot ring reads as decoration at a glance; no city or ATA text, so it says less than S2.

S2

Sello clásico

Classic circular seal

Idea. Text on a circle: TRADUCCIÓN CERTIFICADA, BRIDGE TRADUCCIONES, QUITO, ECUADOR. Globe-B centre and "MIEMBRO ATA" in plain text.

Weakness. Densest option: the circular text is 10 units, about 2 mm at 40 mm. Fine for rubber, tight for a cheap self-inking pad.

S3

Sello notarial

Rectangular notarial stamp

Idea. Rubber-stamp format with fields: Folio Nº, Fecha, Traductor, Firma. The practical one for daily use. Shown at 60 x 35 mm.

Weakness. Fields must be filled by hand or a number stamp; not elegant as a web seal.

S4

Monolínea

Minimal monoline

Idea. One thin ring, a dot arc over the top half, a solid B, CERTIFICADA on the bottom arc.

Weakness. Least information: no name, city or ATA, so it only works beside the printed wordmark.

S5

Arco

Wildcard: arch seal

Idea. An arch (the bridge) with dots peaking at the keystone, globe-B, TRADUCCIÓN CERTIFICADA and a folio line. Shown at 36 mm wide.

Weakness. Non-standard shape: needs a custom rubber die, and some offices expect a round seal.

Recommendation

Main logo: M1, with M5 as its app icon. M1 keeps the strongest link to the 2010 logo: the B in the globe is still the first letter of the name, so existing clients will recognise it at once, and the refresh shows in the cleaner drawing and the new wordmark. M5 covers the favicon and social avatar sizes where a globe cannot hold detail. If you want a bigger step forward, M6 is the strongest new idea.

Stamps: S2 as the official seal, S3 as the working stamp. S2 carries the name, city and ATA membership, which is what a notary or consulate looks for. S3 has the fields (folio, date, translator, signature) needed on every certified page. S1 is a good digital-only seal for the website if you want something lighter there.

Notes and open points

  • Trail colour is not final. The system rules allow amber only on dark surfaces, since it is too faint on white (2.2:1) and on the blue globe (2.8:1). On light surfaces the final trail will be white inside the globe, as in the original, or blue outside it. These sketches show amber everywhere so the trail is easy to spot.
  • The certification text on the document mock is placeholder wording. The legal wording must come from Bridge, because ATA certification does not by itself give a translation legal standing in Ecuador.
  • The original trail is a scatter of about 14 irregular ovals in two streams. Here it is simplified to one curve of 5 to 7 graded dots. Say if you want the original two-stream scatter kept.
  • On Tinta the blue disc is only 2.4:1 against the background. M4 is the dark-native option; M1, M2, M5 and M6 would need a lighter blue or an outline for dark use.
  • The ATA is written as plain text only. The ATA logo is a trademark and is not drawn.
  • Descriptor is set in capitals in each language. Greek capitals drop the accent (ΜΕΤΑΦΡΑΣΕΙΣ), as is correct.
  • Wordmark is Manrope 800 as live text. Final art needs it converted to outlines and the letter fit checked by hand.
