# Audit B: Visual (Bridge Traducciones)

## 1) Why it reads cheap (ranked)
1. **No type system.** Demo hero H1 is Poppins 900 (five stacked lines, one word underlined), body is Inter/Roboto, nav is system/Arial, "Get in Touch" is English in a Spanish site, and a serif italic ("The Bridge Method") appears further down. Four voices, no hierarchy logic. Old site has the same disease: Playfair-like serif H1 + Poppins CTA + Montserrat nav.
2. **Hero is a 5-line, 900-weight shout over a small photo.** Headline owns half the fold at the same weight as the stats; the underlined "negocios" is a styled accent word. Copy is generic ("Traducción profesional... estándares de calidad excepcionales").
3. **Imagery abuse.** Hero photo has a hard offset black drop shadow (reads 2005 PowerPoint), rounded corners, and the globe stamped bottom-right as a sticker. A black empty video block (~450px) sits mid-page in the full screenshot. Old site: purple overlay turns a white-background video into mud and ghosts the logo behind the headline.
4. **Template tells.** Emoji pill ("🟡 Más de 17 años"), centered 3-stat strip (17+/750+/20+) on pale blue, line-icon feature grid, 3-step icon row, "Organizaciones que confían" heading with no logos under it. Unverified claims (750+) hurt credibility for notarial buyers.
5. **Color without a system.** Navy button, #FFD800 yellow nav CTA, orange footer CTA, periwinkle pale-blue bands, bright blue links: three CTA colors on one page. Yellow has zero basis in the logo's blue/grey/black and cheapens a legal-trust brand.
6. **Rhythm is flat.** Sections alternate white / pale-blue bands at similar heights, all centered or all 3-col; no scale change, no large-gap vs small-gap hierarchy. Footer is a WordPress sitemap ("Pricing Plans", "We are Hiring", "App Features", "CreativeThemes" credit) that contradicts the business.
7. **Logo used as-is, tiny, low-res.** The raster logo in the nav is blurry, with the tagline unreadable at nav size; two competing globes (logo + sticker).
8. **Asset mismatch.** The AI "Services Hero" (dark navy, headset call-center, glowing map arcs) is the stock "tech/BPO" cliché and contradicts the real-booth photo; flyer images carry emoji and cream boxes.

## 2) Logo equity
**Keep:** the globe (blue sphere with meridian lines), the dotted trail rising diagonally into the B (this is the "bridge/movement" idea and the ownable shape), the white B knocked out of the globe overlapping the wordmark, the blue family (#1C61AD / #12489F / #0060B6), grey #808285 secondary, "Your Language Partners" tagline.
**Drop:** radial-gradient chrome on "ridge" (dated 2000s bevel), the white-to-black blotchy highlights, thin globe line-work too fine to survive small sizes, "TRANSLATIONS" squeezed under the B at tiny size, italic light-blue tagline stuck in the corner, heavy rounded Century-Gothic-black feel.
**Modernize:** flat or very subtle single-step gradient globe; dotted trail as clean uniform-to-tapered circles on a precise arc; B geometry cleaned and optically balanced; wordmark in solid ink (deep navy or near-black) in one modern geometric/humanist sans; lockups: horizontal, stacked, icon-only, one-color, reversed; icon must work at 16-32px (favicon, seal, notarial stamp).

## 3) Photography verdict
- **Brand-worthy:** `interpeting booth 2.png` (real interpreters, warm glass light, authentic; remove the pasted globe, crop, upscale carefully) and `Interpreting Booth Hero image.png` (strong portrait, blue world-map screen echoes brand; verify it is licensed/AI before use and disclose internally). These two carry the interpreting half.
- **Mediocre/usable secondary:** pen-on-document stock (legal half; needs a real counterpart, e.g. a stamped certified document in hand), IMG_2019 event shots (crop out third-party banners; archive-grade).
- **Reject:** Services Hero (navy sci-fi call center), flyers (emoji, cream box, low-res), hand-with-globe stock banners.
- **Unifying treatment:** consistent cool-neutral grade with a blue shadow tint (#12489F-ish duotone-lite, not an overlay), same aspect ratios (3:2 and 4:5), square or one fixed small radius, no shadows, no stickers. Legal half needs a photo shoot of real documents/seals to reach parity.

## 4) Quality-bar rules for the brand kit
1. Max two typefaces (one display, one text), both with full Spanish diacritics; fixed scale of 5 sizes; H1 at most 2-3 lines, weight 600 not 900.
2. Palette: one ground, one ink, brand blue as the only accent; no yellow/orange; grey #808285 for secondary only; contrast checked AA.
3. One CTA style, one color, everywhere.
4. Logo supplied as vector with clear-space, min-size rules, one-color and reversed versions; never placed on photos as a sticker.
5. Photography: single grade, fixed ratios, no drop shadows, no overlays on faces, no emoji/stock-tech clichés; only verified real or licensed imagery.
6. Both audiences get equal weight: paired entry points (Legal/certificadas, Eventos/interpretación) with equal visual mass.
7. No unverifiable claims or template widgets (pill badges, stat strips without source, empty "clients" headings); copy fully in Spanish with ES/EN/other toggles, no mixed-language UI.
8. Spacing on a fixed scale; section gaps clearly larger than internal gaps; vary composition between adjacent sections.
